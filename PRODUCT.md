# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Sydney phone owners whose device is dead, drowned or "unrepairable" and who need the photos and data off it; plus repair-shop owners who want to outsource board-level work. They arrive stressed (data loss) or sceptical (they have been told no by other shops). The job: convince them within one scroll that this lab does chip-level work in-house and get them to book or call.

## Product Purpose
Marketing homepage for Gadget Data Recovery, the board-level repair and data-recovery lab run out of Sydney Mobile Phone Repairs in Kingsgrove. Success = a booking through the portal, a call, or a trade enquiry. It is the front door for a service most shops cannot offer.

## Brand Personality
Precise, unflinching, hands-on. Three words: microscope, copper, certainty. The voice says "when every other shop says no" without shouting. Amber bench-light on near-black, monospace readouts as instrument labels, imagery of real board work.

## Anti-references
- Boxed card grids with borders (George rejected this layout on 2026-09-06: "I don't like this layout"; photos must fade into the page, no borders).
- Generic phone-repair franchise sites: white background, stock photo of a smiling tech, blue buttons.
- Crypto/AI-style gradient heroes.

## Design Principles
- Show the bench, not the brochure: every image is a real subject from the lab (CPU on tweezers, labelled board, die under the scope, reballed chip, split board layers).
- Instrument, not decoration: monospace is used only as readouts and labels tied to the work (magnification, chip names), never as a costume.
- Motion explains the repair: callouts draw onto the board, balls reflow, layers separate. Nothing moves that does not describe what the lab does.
- Expert confidence: "No fix, no fee" and "in-house, nothing sent away" are the two claims the page keeps returning to.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast on the dark ground. Full prefers-reduced-motion path: every scene resolves to its final frame, no autoplay. Keyboard-visible focus on all links. Content readable with JavaScript disabled.
