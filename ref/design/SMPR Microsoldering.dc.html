WHAT WE REPAIR

Repairs most shops send away — done in-house

Every job below is performed at our Kingsgrove bench under microscope, with a 3-month warranty on parts and labour.

01

Data recovery

Dead phone, water damage, won't turn on — your photos and files are usually still recoverable. We repair the board just enough to boot and pull your data. No recovery, no fee.

02

Microsoldering

Component-level diagnosis and repair: charging ICs, audio ICs, backlight circuits, touch faults, shorted lines and liquid damage. We fix the fault, not just swap parts.

03

CPU & NAND swaps

Chip-off transfers of CPU and NAND between boards — the last-resort repair that saves a device and its data when the board itself is beyond economical repair.

04

Board layer separation & swaps

Modern iPhone sandwich boards can be split, and a good layer married to a donor. One of the few Sydney shops doing layer swaps in-house.

05

Face ID repairs

Dot projector and flood illuminator faults repaired at component level — restoring original Face ID without replacing the whole phone.

06

Rear camera repairs

Shaking, blurry or black rear cameras repaired — including component-level fixes that keep your original camera and its calibration.

07

Laptop & MacBook microsoldering

Logic board repair for MacBooks and Windows laptops: no power, liquid damage, charging faults and dead GPUs — repaired, not replaced.
