WHY BOARD-LEVEL MATTERS

Modern phones encrypt data to the board itself

On modern iPhones and Samsungs, the storage chip is encrypted and paired to the CPU. You can't just "read the chip" — software recovery tools are useless on a dead board.

The only way in is board repair: fix the fault, or transplant the paired CPU and storage together onto a donor board. That's microsoldering work — and it's what we do all day.
