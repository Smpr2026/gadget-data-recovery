CPU & NAND swaps: the last-resort repair

A CPU swap means lifting the processor and storage from a dead board and transplanting them onto a donor, under the microscope, ball by ball. It's the repair that saves a device and its data when nothing else can.

Because it takes many hours of careful bench work and carries real risk on badly damaged boards, it's quoted as a premium service: we assess the board first, give you a firm price and honest odds, and you decide before we start.

  • Assessed and quoted before any work starts
  • Allow extra turnaround. This is hours of microscope work, not a same-day job
  • Priced for the time and skill involved, with no surprises after the quote
